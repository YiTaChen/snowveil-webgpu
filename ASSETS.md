# Asset register

## Authored visual assets

There are no imported runtime art assets. The playable visual is fully original
and generated by project code at runtime:

- terrain, atmosphere, snow material, particles, HDR resolve, deformation, and
  Ice Pulse shading in `app/snowveil-shader.ts`;
- rider, articulated limb groups, cloth, knee guards, boots, bindings,
  asymmetric directional blade nose, and upturned snow-surfing blade geometry in
  `app/snowveil-rider-geometry.ts`;
- frost-sigil plinth, crystal, ring, snow cap, and support-fin geometry in
  `app/snowveil-beacon-geometry.ts`;
- deterministic wind, board hiss, jump/landing transients, rumble, spell tones,
  beacon drones, and completion chord synthesis in `app/snowveil-audio.ts`;
- edge/skid-driven powder mist and ballistic flecks in
  `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, plus a separate synthesized high-band edge scrape in
  `app/snowveil-audio.ts`; neither path uses an image, simulation cache, or
  recorded sound;
- CPU terrain contact and slope sampling in `app/snowveil-terrain.ts`;
- terrain-projected snowboard acceleration, skid-derived braking, tested
  board/contact-axis helpers, and authored rider pose-transition envelopes in
  `app/snowveil-motion.ts`;
- state-weighted knee loading and the spell-gated forearm hierarchy in
  `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, authored for this project without a clip, skeleton,
  motion-capture sample, or external animation runtime;
- sightline-aware beacon transparency and its render ordering in
  `app/snowveil-shader.ts` and `app/snowveil-scene.tsx`;
- split goggle optics, mask, vents, hinges, glove thumbs, and hand-back geometry
  authored procedurally in `app/snowveil-rider-geometry.ts`, with original
  material and hierarchy logic in `app/snowveil-shader.ts`;
- the allocation-free four-link cape/scarf response in
  `app/snowveil-motion.ts`, `app/snowveil-scene.tsx`, and
  `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, driven only by project-owned world wind and live
  rider motion rather than an animation clip or cloth asset;
- 768 terrain-coupled spindrift ribbons in `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, placed by
  project-authored WGSL from deterministic hashes, the existing terrain field,
  and persistent snow history; the effect uses no sprite, scan, texture,
  simulation cache, footage, or third-party particle asset;
- the directional Ice Pulse impact crown in `app/snowveil-rider-geometry.ts`
  and `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, built from project-authored broken arc geometry,
  asymmetric crystal splinters, a camera-projected hand-to-impact filament, and
  the existing procedural snow deformation; it uses no sprite, decal, texture,
  model, animation clip, footage, scan, or third-party effect asset;
- sun-coupled rider, board, jump, and frost-beacon shadows in
  `app/snowveil-shader.ts`, evaluated analytically from project-owned world
  positions, heights, and yaw in the terrain vertex stage; they use no shadow
  texture, baked light map, scan, image, model, or external lighting asset;
- forty-eight terrain-coupled ski-area safety poles, warm reflector bands,
  snowy caps, sagging rope spans, and hanging pennants in
  `app/snowveil-boundary-geometry.ts`, with original world lighting, fog, and
  material treatment in `app/snowveil-shader.ts`; no model, mesh, texture,
  decal, logo, scan, footage, or third-party resort marking is used;
- responsive HUD and touch-control markup, glyph treatment, and glass styling
  in `app/snowveil-scene.tsx` and `app/globals.css`.

The files under `evidence/` are direct captures of this running build. They are
review artefacts, not external source material or runtime assets.

## Authored social-preview asset

`public/og.png` is a 1200×630 social card generated once with OpenAI's built-in
image generator. Its only visual reference was this project's own running-build
capture, `evidence/performance-rite-after-2560x1440.png`. The prompt requested an
original Snowveil promotional composition, the project title, and the subtitle
`FROST RITE · WEBGPU`; it did not name an artist, franchise, protected character,
or third-party game. The image was visually checked for text accuracy, unwanted
marks, and consistency with the project-owned rider, beacons, and palette. It is
used for link previews only and is not sampled by the renderer.

## Runtime and build dependencies

The application uses React, vinext, Vite, TypeScript, and Cloudflare build tools.
Their licence notices remain in the installed packages and lockfile; the audited
direct-dependency table and special transitive licence families are recorded in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](./THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). No third-party dependency
supplies the snow appearance, terrain, geometry, animation, branding, or sound.

Any future addition must be registered here with source, author, licence, files,
modifications, and attribution requirements before it enters a release.
