# Asset register

## Authored visual assets

There are no imported runtime art assets. The playable visual is fully original
and generated by project code at runtime:

- terrain, atmosphere, snow material, particles, HDR resolve, deformation, and
  Ice Pulse shading in `app/snowveil-shader.ts`;
- rider, cloth, boots, bindings, asymmetric directional blade nose, and upturned
  snow-surfing blade geometry in
  `app/snowveil-rider-geometry.ts`;
- frost-sigil plinth, crystal, ring, snow cap, and support-fin geometry in
  `app/snowveil-beacon-geometry.ts`;
- deterministic wind, board hiss, jump/landing transients, rumble, spell tones,
  beacon drones, and completion chord synthesis in `app/snowveil-audio.ts`;
- CPU terrain contact and slope sampling in `app/snowveil-terrain.ts`;
- terrain-projected snowboard acceleration, skid-derived braking, and tested
  board/contact-axis helpers in `app/snowveil-motion.ts`;
- responsive HUD and touch-control markup, glyph treatment, and glass styling
  in `app/snowveil-scene.tsx` and `app/globals.css`.

The files under `evidence/` are direct captures of this running build. They are
review artefacts, not external source material or runtime assets.

## Authored social-preview asset

`public/og.png` is a 1200×630 social card generated once with OpenAI's built-in
image generator. Its only visual reference was this project's own running-build
capture, `evidence/performance-rite-after-2560x1440.png`. The prompt requested an
original Snowveil promotional composition, the project title, and the subtitle
`FROST RITE · WEBGPU`; it did not name an artist, franchise, protected character,
or third-party game. The image was visually checked for text accuracy, unwanted
marks, and consistency with the project-owned rider, beacons, and palette. It is
used for link previews only and is not sampled by the renderer.

## Runtime and build dependencies

The application uses React, vinext, Vite, TypeScript, and Cloudflare build tools.
Their licence notices remain in the installed packages and lockfile; the audited
direct-dependency table and special transitive licence families are recorded in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](./THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md). No third-party dependency
supplies the snow appearance, terrain, geometry, animation, branding, or sound.

Any future addition must be registered here with source, author, licence, files,
modifications, and attribution requirements before it enters a release.
